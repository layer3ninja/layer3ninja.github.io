# Layer3Ninja: Network Engineering From the Field

Source for **[layer3ninja.github.io](https://layer3ninja.github.io)**, by Farshad, a network
engineer based in Vancouver, Canada.

The site collects real incidents I've worked on and the people skills that make
troubleshooting work:

- **Field Stories**: real production incidents. What broke, how I found it, and what I
  learned fixing it.
- **layer8-softskills**: the layer above the OSI model. Communication, psychology, learning
  and mindset at work.

## Why I write this

There are two reasons. The first is **to learn**. Turning an incident into a clear write-up
forces me to understand *why* something broke, not just how I fixed it, and that's what
makes the lesson stick.

The second is **to share**. Most of what I know, I learned from engineers who took the time
to write things down. If one of these stories saves someone an hour of troubleshooting, or a
long night during a change window, it was worth writing.

Every story comes from real situations I've worked through. Company names, hostnames and IP
addresses are changed or removed to protect the networks involved.
