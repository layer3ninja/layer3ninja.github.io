# Network Engineering, From the Field

Hi, I'm **Farshad**, a network engineer based in Vancouver, Canada. This site collects
real incidents I've worked on and the people skills that make troubleshooting work.
Everything here is practical: real commands, real outputs, real lessons.

## Why I write this

There are two reasons. The first is **to learn**. Turning an incident into a clear write-up
forces me to understand *why* something broke, not just how I fixed it, and that's what
makes the lesson stick.

The second is **to share**. Most of what I know, I learned from engineers who took the time
to write things down. If one of these stories saves someone an hour of troubleshooting, or a
long night during a change window, it was worth writing.

!!! info "Do I use AI to write these posts?"
    Yes, of course. Typing every post from scratch would be a waste of time, so I use AI to
    help with structure, wording and diagrams. **But the content is mine:** every story comes
    from my own experience and real situations I've worked through. Company names, hostnames
    and IP addresses are changed or removed to protect the networks involved.
